📖 Comparatives & superlatives

Two things → comparative + than. Three or more → the + superlative.

Short adjectives: + er / + est

fast → faster than → the fastest · old → older → the oldest

Ending in -e: + r / + st

wide → wider → the widest · large → larger

Vowel + consonant: double it

big → bigger → the biggest · hot → hotter

Ending in -y: y → ier / iest

noisy → noisier → the noisiest · heavy → heavier

Long adjectives: more / the most

expensive → more expensive than → the most expensive

Irregular!

good → better → the best · bad → worse → the worst

The opposite: less

The hill is less dangerous than the mountain.
